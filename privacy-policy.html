Privacy Policy for Fetch Productivity Suite

Last updated: November 2026 Maintained by: Fetch Rewards Fraud Operations

What this extension does

The Fetch Productivity Suite is an internal productivity tool used exclusively by Fetch Rewards fraud analysts. It tracks time spent on fraud review tasks and logs fraud case activity to an internal Google Sheet managed by the Fraud Operations team. It is not distributed to, or intended for, the general public.

What data is collected

The extension collects the following data:

  • Fetch Google account email address: read when the analyst connects their Fetch Google Workspace account, and used only to confirm which employee submitted each record
  • Analyst display name: entered by the analyst in the extension settings, or filled in automatically from the team roster once their Fetch account is connected
  • Time tracking data: activity name, duration, start time, and end time for work sessions
  • Scheduled shift hours and days off: entered by the analyst, and used for shift-end alerts and overtime calculations
  • Fraud case activity: case or ticket IDs, platform (Kount, Dispute Review in Zendesk, or Vector Review in Zendesk), Kount event type (Fraud Platform or Review), and handle time for cases reviewed during a session
  • Date and timestamp of each activity
  • Sync/connection status: used only to confirm data successfully reached the internal backend

What data is NOT collected

  • Passwords or login credentials (Google sign-in is handled by Chrome and Google; the extension never sees the analyst's password)
  • Any Google account data other than the email address: no contacts, files, mail, or calendar
  • Browsing history: the extension only recognizes the work sites it tracks time for (the fraud review platforms, the team dashboard, and approved training and work tools) and records the activity name, not page addresses
  • Financial information
  • Location data
  • Health information

How data is used

All collected data is used solely for internal workforce productivity reporting within Fetch Rewards. Specifically:

  • Time tracking and shift data are used to generate productivity and overtime reports for fraud analysts and their supervisors
  • Case activity data is used to calculate average handle time and case volume metrics
  • The email address is used to verify that each record came from the employee it says it came from
  • Sync status data is used only to alert the analyst or admin to connection issues
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising
  • No data is used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes

Where data is stored

Data is transmitted over HTTPS to a Google Apps Script endpoint and stored in a Google Sheet accessible only to authorized Fetch Rewards Fraud Operations team members. All data is stored within Google's infrastructure. Some working data is kept only in the analyst's own browser (for example, in-progress timers, settings, and saves waiting to sync) so nothing is lost on a page refresh. A local JSON backup option is also available to the analyst as a manual recovery mechanism in case of sync failure; this backup is downloaded to the analyst's own device and is not transmitted elsewhere.

Who has access

Only Fetch Rewards Fraud Operations team members with explicit access to the internal Google Sheet can view collected data. Access is managed by the Fraud Operations team administrator. Within the extension's dashboard, analysts can only view their own data; supervisors and admins can view data for the full team.

Data retention

Data is retained indefinitely for historical reporting purposes. Analysts who leave the team have their historical data preserved for reporting accuracy, but are hidden from active/current team views.

Permissions this extension uses, and why

  • tabs: to detect which work tab is currently active, so time tracking applies to the correct task
  • storage: to save time-tracking state, settings, and unsent records locally so an in-progress session isn't lost on a page refresh
  • alarms: to periodically check sync status and refresh timers in the background
  • notifications: to alert the analyst locally for shift, break, and lunch reminders, and if a sync fails and needs attention
  • downloads: to allow the analyst to save a local JSON backup of their tracked data as a manual recovery option
  • idle: to notice when the computer is idle or locked, so time isn't counted while the analyst is away
  • identity: to sign the analyst in with their Fetch Google Workspace account (email scope only) so the backend can verify who submitted each record
  • Host permissions (Zendesk, Kount, script.google.com): required so the extension can read case data from those specific fraud platforms and sync it to the internal Google Sheet backend. No other websites' content is read.

This extension does not use remote code execution; all logic ships within the extension package itself.
